# First to Achieve – Turn Every Advancement into a Race!

What if every achievement in Minecraft became a high-stakes competition?

First to Achieve transforms the way you play by turning advancements into a true race. The moment a player unlocks an achievement, it’s permanently claimed—no one else on the server can obtain it again. Every milestone becomes a battle for speed, strategy, and skill.

Will you rush to secure the rarest advancements, or outsmart your rivals and claim victory where it matters most?

- 🏁 One Winner Only – Each advancement can be earned by a single player.
- ⚔️ Competitive Gameplay – Every action counts in the race to be first.
- 🧠 Strategic Decisions – Choose your path wisely before others beat you to it.
- 🌍 Perfect for Multiplayer Servers – Create intense, unforgettable competition with friends.

Redefine progress. Claim your legacy. Be the first… or miss out forever.
